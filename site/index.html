From bad habits to a sharp policy

A tiny-parameter transformer learns a key-and-door maze. SFT on demos from a planner with a bad habit, then two ways to fix it: GRPO (online RL on a verifiable reward) and DPO (offline, on preference pairs). A fourth run shows what happens when the reward is subtly wrong. Results are averaged over several seeds, and the demo runs the trained weights in your browser.

Live race: same level, four policies

Each panel runs a checkpoint on the same maze, in your browser. Change a panel's checkpoint from its title. Pick up the key, go through the door, reach the goal within 30 steps. Reward = 1 − 0.01 × steps on success, otherwise 0.

Training scrubber rewinds the GRPO and Reward-hacker panels to a training snapshot (iteration 0 = SFT)

Click any interior cell in any panel to toggle a wall. Unsolvable mazes are refused.

Eval suite

Fixed held-out sets: test (same distribution as training) and hard (denser walls, never trained on). Cells show the mean over seeds, with ± one standard deviation. pass@8 is the unbiased estimator from 8 samples; sampled is the average success of those samples. Proxy is the hackable closeness reward. (Efficiency, optimal ÷ taken steps, is 1.00 for every checkpoint: when greedy decoding succeeds it takes the shortest route, so it is left out.)
